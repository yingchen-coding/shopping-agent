# Unknown dimension scores are excluded from the score denominator

Search results are routinely missing the information a dimension needs — material composition and
age grading are absent far more often than they are present. Scoring a missing dimension as zero
would rank Products by *how completely a merchant filled in their description* rather than by
how well they suit the household, so Unknown is instead excluded from the weighted average
entirely and rendered as Unknown wherever the score is shown.

Unknown is therefore a distinct third value, not a low score and not a passing one — treating it as
either would let absent evidence stand in for real evidence, in one direction or the other.

## Consequences

A product with one known dimension can outrank one with five, because the first is averaged over a
smaller, kinder denominator. This is why the per-dimension breakdown is mandatory rather than
optional output: the score alone is genuinely misleading without it, and a reader must be able to see
that a high score rests on thin evidence.
